# Day 6 — 2-Week Instagram/Facebook Content Calendar (Deep Dive Trips)

**Note on method:** `business-info.md` still doesn't exist in the repo, so trip names, dates,
locations, and prices below are bracketed placeholders — swap in real specifics before shooting
or posting. Separately, and regardless of whether placeholders are filled in: **Instagram and
Facebook posts are always draft-only under the standing rule** — this routine will never publish
to either platform automatically, no matter how complete the copy is. Everything below is for a
human to review, shoot, and post manually (or hand to a scheduler like Meta Business Suite/Later).

Reels-first, per the task brief — Instagram and Reels/Facebook video consistently outperform
static posts for engagement and reach on both platforms right now, and dive/adventure content is
exactly the category that benefits most (movement, scenery, a clear "would I do this" hook).

---

## How to read this calendar

- **14 posts, one per day, 2 weeks.** Each entry: format, hook/caption, shot list, CTA.
- **Reel entries** describe the shot sequence to film — most can be captured on a phone during a
  normal trip, no separate production day needed.
- **Static/carousel entries** are for days without new footage, or for content that works better
  as a photo dump (reviews, before/after, gear).
- Post once per day, same platforms (IG feed/Reels + cross-post to Facebook Page), ideally between
  6-9pm IST when people are off work and browsing — adjust once real IG/FB insights are available
  (blocked on Windsor plan limit / Instagram not yet connected in Windsor, both already flagged in
  TASKS.md).
- Every caption stays under ~150 words with one clear CTA and 8-15 relevant hashtags (mix of
  broad + niche + location, listed once at the bottom of this file to reuse across posts rather
  than repeating per post).

## Week 1

### 1. Mon — Reel: "POV: your first dive"
**Hook:** "POV: it's your first time breathing underwater 🤿"
**Shot list:** Boat departure → gearing-up hands/checks → giant-step or backward roll entry →
5-10 sec of descent, diver's POV → a beat of reef/marine life → ascent + big smile breaking
surface. Cut to the beat of trending audio, 15-20 sec total.
**Caption:** "POV: it's your first time breathing underwater 🤿 No experience needed — we brief
you, we're right next to you the whole time, and this is what 10 minutes into your first dive
actually looks like. [Course/discovery dive name] running this week at [Location]. Drop a 🌊 if
this is going on your bucket list, or DM/WhatsApp us to book."
**CTA:** DM or WhatsApp to book.

### 2. Tue — Carousel: "What's actually in the price"
**Hook:** "Booking a dive trip for the first time? Here's exactly what you're paying for →"
**Shot list:** Slide 1: title card. Slides 2-5: gear rack, boat, instructor briefing, certificate/
logbook stamp — one photo per included item.
**Caption:** "New to booking dive trips? Here's exactly what's included in [trip/course name]:
[gear rental, instructor/guide, boat transfer, certification fee if applicable — list 3-4 real
items]. [Anything not included, e.g. meals/hotel transfer, if relevant]. No surprise costs at
check-in. Questions? Ask below or WhatsApp us."
**CTA:** Comment or WhatsApp with questions.

### 3. Wed — Reel: Marine life close-up
**Hook:** "[Location] on a good visibility day 🐢"
**Shot list:** 10-15 sec of the best marine-life footage available (turtle/reef shark/manta/reef
scene — whatever is actually common at the dive site) with a diver briefly in frame for scale,
then a quick surface/boat cutaway.
**Caption:** "[Location] on a good visibility day 🐢 This is what's waiting under the surface,
[X] minutes from [nearest town/landmark]. Best conditions right now: [season/month note]. Tag
someone who needs to see this."
**CTA:** Tag a friend / save this post.

### 4. Thu — Static: Myth-busting
**Hook:** "'You need to be a strong swimmer to dive' — actually, no."
**Shot list:** Single strong photo — diver comfortably floating/hovering, calm expression, not a
mid-action shot (supports the "it's not as intense as you think" message).
**Caption:** "'You need to be a strong swimmer to dive' — actually, no. You need to be comfortable
in water, that's it. Your gear includes a buoyancy vest that keeps you afloat with almost no
effort, and we assess your comfort level before anyone goes below the surface. If [specific
worry, e.g. fear of deep water] has been holding you back, tell us — we've heard it before and
we'll work with it."
**CTA:** "Tell us your biggest hesitation in the comments — we'll answer honestly."

### 5. Fri — Reel: Behind the scenes
**Hook:** "What actually happens before you get in the water"
**Shot list:** Gear check montage → briefing → dive site prep/anchor → team getting geared up.
Casual, not overly polished — authenticity outperforms production value for this angle.
**Caption:** "What happens before you ever get in the water: gear check, full safety briefing, dive
plan confirmed with everyone. This is the part people don't see but it's the reason nothing goes
wrong once you're under. Team behind every dive at Deep Dive Trips."
**CTA:** "Follow along for more of what goes on behind the scenes."

### 6. Sat — Carousel: Customer story
**Hook:** "[Customer first name/initial] had never dived before this trip."
**Shot list:** Before shot (nervous/on the boat) + after shot (post-dive smile/high-five), 2-4
slides max.
**Caption:** "[Customer name/initial] came in [nervous / never dived before], left [certified /
already asking about the next trip]. [Optional 1-line real quote if the customer gave permission
to share]. That reaction is exactly what we're chasing every time. Ready for yours? WhatsApp us to
book [trip/course name]."
**CTA:** WhatsApp to book.

### 7. Sun — Reel: Weekly recap / trip highlight
**Hook:** "This week at Deep Dive Trips 🌊"
**Shot list:** Fast-cut compilation of the week's best clips across all trips run — 3-5 short
clips, upbeat trending audio, end card with next available date.
**Caption:** "This week at Deep Dive Trips 🌊 [1 line on what happened — new certifications,
group trip, standout sighting]. Next dates open: [upcoming date/trip]. Link in bio / WhatsApp to
grab a spot."
**CTA:** Link in bio / WhatsApp.

## Week 2

### 8. Mon — Reel: Group/certification trip
**Hook:** "Bringing your friends beats going alone — here's why"
**Shot list:** Group of divers on the boat, geared up together, high-fiving pre- or post-dive.
**Caption:** "Bringing [3+ friends]? [Group discount/private group booking] available for
[course/trip name] — book together, dive together. Tag your dive squad below." *(Only post if a
real group offer exists — do not invent a discount that isn't confirmed by the client.)*
**CTA:** Tag your group / enquire for group rates.

### 9. Tue — Static: Q&A repost from GMB
**Hook:** "Most asked question this month:"
**Shot list:** Simple text-on-image graphic, one question per post — reuse the Day 5 GMB Q&A
content (`day5-gmb-posts-week2-qa.md`) so the same real answers stay consistent everywhere once
`business-info.md` fills them in.
**Caption:** Pull directly from the matching Day 5 Q&A answer once real specifics exist — keeps
the answer identical across GMB, Instagram, and Facebook rather than drifting.
**CTA:** "Have a different question? Ask below."

### 10. Wed — Reel: Instructor/team spotlight
**Hook:** "Meet [Instructor name], [X] years teaching people to dive"
**Shot list:** Instructor talking briefly to camera (5-10 sec) + b-roll of them teaching/briefing
a group.
**Caption:** "Meet [Instructor name] — [certification level, e.g. PADI Instructor], [X] years
teaching at Deep Dive Trips. [1 line on what they love about teaching or a memorable student
moment]. You'll likely have [him/her/them] on your first dive."
**CTA:** "Comment if you've dived with [Instructor name] before."

### 11. Thu — Carousel: Before you book checklist
**Hook:** "5 things to know before your first dive with us"
**Shot list:** Text-card carousel, 5 slides, one tip per slide (what to bring, what's provided,
timing, fitness level needed, cancellation policy).
**Caption:** "Booking your first dive? Here's what to know: 1) [gear provided vs. bring] 2) [what
to wear/bring — swimwear, towel, reef-safe sunscreen] 3) [arrival time before departure] 4) [no
strong-swimmer requirement — link back to post 4] 5) [real cancellation/reschedule policy]. Save
this before you book."
**CTA:** Save this post.

### 12. Fri — Reel: "What it's really like" myth-bust #2
**Hook:** "'Diving is expensive and only for experienced travelers' — here's the real cost breakdown"
**Shot list:** Quick cuts of the discovery dive experience with price/what's-included text
overlays timed to the cuts.
**Caption:** "Diving isn't reserved for experienced divers or big budgets. [Discovery dive/course
name] starts at [real price, only if the client is comfortable showing it] and includes [gear +
instructor + boat]. First-timers welcome, no certification required to start."
**CTA:** WhatsApp for this week's availability.

### 13. Sat — Static: Social proof compilation
**Hook:** "Some of what people are saying 🌊"
**Shot list:** Screenshot compilation of 3-4 real Google/Facebook reviews (with permission/public
reviews only), formatted as clean text cards, not raw screenshots with usernames unless the
reviewer is fine being named.
**Caption:** "Some of what people are saying after diving with us 🌊 [Optional: 1 line reacting to
a specific standout comment]. Thinking about booking? These are real reviews, not scripted."
**CTA:** "Ready to add yours? WhatsApp us."

### 14. Sun — Reel: Two-week recap + next trip teaser
**Hook:** "Two weeks, this many first-timers certified 🤿"
**Shot list:** Best-of compilation across both weeks — heavier on emotional/reaction shots than
week 1's recap, ending on a teaser for the next upcoming trip/date.
**Caption:** "Two weeks, [X real number if known, else 'a lot of'] first dives, first
certifications, and first "I can't believe I just did that" reactions. Next up: [upcoming trip/
date/location]. Spots are limited — WhatsApp us to lock yours in."
**CTA:** WhatsApp to book.
