# Day 1 — GMB Foundations (Deep Dive Trips)

## GMB completeness checklist
- [ ] Primary category set to the closest exact match (e.g. "Scuba instructor" / "Tour operator" —
      confirm which fits; primary category carries the most local-ranking weight)
- [ ] 2-3 relevant secondary categories added (e.g. adventure sports center, travel agency)
- [ ] Service area set correctly if trips run out of multiple locations
- [ ] Business description (750 char) rewritten to front-load what you do + where, naturally
      including the terms customers search ("scuba diving trips", "diving certification", city names)
- [ ] Primary phone is a number that's actually answered during business hours — calls from GMB
      that ring out are wasted spend on the trust you've built
- [ ] Website URL points to the most relevant landing page, not just the homepage
- [ ] Regular hours + special/holiday hours kept current
- [ ] All applicable attributes filled in (women-led, accepts bookings online, appointment links, etc.)
- [ ] Cover photo + logo are current and high-res
- [ ] At least 10 real photos: trips in action, the team, gear, certifications/credentials,
      before customers even click through — this is the #1 thing people check before booking
      an adventure activity
- [ ] Products/services list populated with each trip/course type + price if you're comfortable
      showing it (removes friction, filters unqualified enquiries)
- [ ] Q&A section seeded with the 5-8 questions you get asked most (safety, experience needed,
      what's included, cancellation policy, group size) — answered by the business, not left open
      for randoms to answer first

## Review-request templates

**WhatsApp (send within 24h of trip end):**
> Hey [Name]! Hope you had an amazing time with us at [Trip/Location] 🌊 If you enjoyed it, a
> quick Google review would mean a lot and helps other divers find us — takes 30 seconds:
> [review link]
> Also, if you snapped any good shots/videos, we'd love to feature you (with credit) — feel free
> to send them over!

**Email (if you collect emails at booking):**
> Subject: How was your dive with Deep Dive Trips? 🤿
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for diving with us at [Trip/Location]! We hope it was unforgettable.
>
> If you have 30 seconds, a Google review helps other travelers find us and helps us keep
> improving: [review link]
>
> See you underwater again soon,
> Team Deep Dive Trips

**Review reply templates:**
- 5-star: "Thank you so much [Name] — so glad you had a great time at [Trip/Location]! Hope to
  see you on your next dive with us 🌊"
- 3-star or below: "Thanks for the honest feedback, [Name] — sorry [specific issue] didn't meet
  expectations. We'd like to make this right, please reach out to us directly at [contact] so we
  can look into it."
  (Always reply within 48h, always take anything negative offline immediately, always sound human not templated)

## Week 1 GMB post calendar (draft — swap in real photos/details before posting)

1. **Mon** — Trip highlight: "This week's dive at [Location] 🐢 [1-2 lines on what made it
   special]. Spots open for [next date] → [CTA: Book now / WhatsApp us]"
2. **Wed** — Educational: "New to diving? Here's what your first certification course actually
   covers [3 bullet points]. No experience needed."
3. **Fri** — Social proof: repost a great review or a customer photo with permission + a line of
   thanks
4. **Sat** — Offer/urgency (if applicable): "Only [X] spots left for [upcoming trip date] —
   book before [date]"
5. **Sun** — Behind the scenes: gear check / team / boat / dive site prep — builds trust in
   safety and professionalism

Each post: <1500 characters, one clear CTA button (Book / Call / Learn more), one strong photo.
