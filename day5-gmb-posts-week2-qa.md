## Week 2 GMB post calendar (draft — swap in real photos/details before posting)

Week 1 covered: trip highlight, educational (course breakdown), social proof/review repost,
offer/urgency, behind-the-scenes. Week 2 keeps the same weekly rhythm (variety > repetition, one
CTA per post, one strong photo per post) but rotates in five different angles so regular
GMB/Search visitors see fresh material:

1. **Mon — Destination spotlight:** "Diving at [Location Name] isn't just about the dive — it's
   [1-2 lines on what makes the site/area itself worth the trip: visibility, marine life density,
   scenery, accessibility]. Best conditions right now: [season/month note]. → [CTA: Check upcoming
   dates / WhatsApp us]"
2. **Wed — Educational (myth-busting):** "'[Common misconception about diving, e.g. "You need to
   be a strong swimmer to dive"]' — actually, [1-2 lines correcting it with what's really required].
   If [specific concern] has been holding you back, ask us — we've heard it before. → [CTA: Message
   us your questions]"
3. **Fri — Customer story (not just a star rating):** Instead of reposting a review verbatim, tell
   a short story version: "[Customer first name/initial] came in [nervous/never dived before/etc.],
   left [certified/hooked/planning their next trip]. That's the reaction we're chasing every time.
   [Optional: 1-line real quote if available]" — pairs with a genuine reaction-shot photo (see Day 4
   shot list #5).
4. **Sat — Group/certification offer:** "Bringing [3+ / a group of] friends? [Group discount /
   private group booking] available for [course/trip name] — book together, dive together. → [CTA:
   Enquire for group rates]" (Only post if a real group offer exists — do not invent a discount
   that isn't real.)
5. **Sun — What's included (removes friction):** "Booking [trip/course name]? Here's exactly what's
   covered: [gear/instructor/boat/certification fee/etc. — bullet 3-4 items] and what's not
   [meals/transport/etc. if applicable]. No surprise costs. → [CTA: See full details / Book now]"

Each post: <1500 characters, one clear CTA button (Book / Call / Learn more), one strong photo —
same format constraint as Week 1, since that's a GMB post-length/engagement best practice, not a
Week 1-only rule.

**Sequencing note:** Post Week 2 only after Week 1 has actually run (or in whatever order fits the
real calendar) — the numbering here is "second batch of 5," not tied to specific calendar dates

## Q&A seed questions

Day 1's completeness checklist flagged this as a gap: *"Q&A section seeded with the 5-8 questions
you get asked most... answered by the business, not left open for randoms to answer first."* These
are ready to post as both the question and the business's own answer (Google lets the business
account post both sides — always seed it this way so a competitor or troll doesn't get to answer
first). Swap bracketed specifics for real ones before posting; the framing and structure don't need
real data to be usable as-is except where marked.

1. **Q: Do I need diving experience to book a trip?**
   A: Not for our beginner/discovery dives — no experience needed, we start with a full briefing
   and shallow-water practice before anything else. If you want to get certified, [course name]
   is designed for complete first-timers too. Let us know your experience level when you enquire
   and we'll recommend the right option.

2. **Q: Is diving safe if I'm not a strong swimmer?**
   A: Yes — you don't need to be a strong swimmer, just comfortable in water. All dives are guided
   by [certified instructors / a dive master], gear includes a buoyancy vest that keeps you afloat
   effortlessly, and we assess comfort in the water before anyone goes below the surface.

3. **Q: What's included in the price?**
   A: [List what's included: gear rental, instructor/guide, boat transfer, certification fee if
   applicable — bullet or comma list]. [List anything not included, if relevant: meals, hotel
   transfers, etc.] Full breakdown is on our [website page / send us a message] before you book.

4. **Q: What's your cancellation/rescheduling policy?**
   A: [Real policy — e.g. "Free rescheduling up to X hours before the trip; cancellations within Y
   hours are non-refundable due to boat/gear booking commitments." Do not invent a policy — pull
   from business-info.md or ask the client directly.]

5. **Q: How many people are typically in a group?**
   A: We keep group sizes to [real max ratio, e.g. "1 instructor per 4 divers"] so everyone gets
   proper attention and safety supervision — we don't pack boats to maximize headcount.

6. **Q: Do you offer certification courses, or just one-off dives?**
   A: [Answer based on real offering — e.g. "Both. [Discovery dive name] is a one-time guided dive
   with no certification, and [certification course name] is a full [PADI/SSI] open water course
   over [X days] if you want to be certified to dive anywhere in the world."]

7. **Q: What should I bring / do I need my own gear?**
   A: All core gear (mask, fins, wetsuit, tank, regulator, BCD) is provided and maintained by us —
   just bring swimwear, a towel, and sunscreen (reef-safe if you have it). If you own personal gear
   and prefer to use it, that's fine too.

8. **Q: Where do trips depart from / how do I get there?**
   A: [Real departure point and any pickup arrangement — e.g. "Trips depart from [location/jetty
   name]. We can arrange pickup from [nearby landmark/hotel area] if needed — mention this when you
   book."]

**Posting note:** GMB Q&A is publicly visible and can be upvoted/edited by anyone, including the
business. Seed all 8 as business-posted Q&As (not left for organic questions to trickle in), and
check monthly for new questions from real users that need an answer before someone else answers
incorrectly.
