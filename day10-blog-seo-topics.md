## Tier 1 — Top-of-funnel / informational (write first, no business-info.md needed)

### 1. Is scuba diving safe for beginners? What first-timers actually need to know
- **Target keyword:** is scuba diving safe for beginners
- **Secondary:** scuba diving safety tips, is scuba diving dangerous
- **Intent:** informational (early research, high anxiety/objection-handling value)
- **Title tag:** `Is Scuba Diving Safe for Beginners? What to Know | Deep Dive Trips`
- **Meta description:** `Scuba diving is one of the safest adventure sports when done right. Here's what actually makes a first dive safe — and what to check before you book.`
- **Outline:** the real safety stats/framing (diving is statistically safer than driving to the dive site when instructor-led); what a Discover Scuba Dive briefing actually covers; buddy system + instructor ratio; equipment checks; what medical conditions to disclose beforehand; how to vet an operator (certification, insurance, group size) — this section is the natural pivot to "what to look for," which doubles as a soft trust-builder for Deep Dive Trips without being a hard sell.
- **Word count:** 900-1,200
- **Internal links:** → certification course page (Day 7, Page 3), → GMB Q&A / reviews for social proof
- **CTA:** `[Still have questions? WhatsApp us →]` (`click_whatsapp`, `cta_location: in_content`)

- **Title tag:** `What to Expect on Your First Scuba Dive | Deep Dive Trips`
- **Meta description:** `Never been diving before? Here's exactly what happens from check-in to your first breath underwater — no surprises, no jargon.`

- **Word count:** 800-1,000
- **Internal links:** → flagship destination page (Day 7, Page 1), → certification course page

### 5. Can you scuba dive if you can't swim well? What the real requirement is
- **Target keyword:** can you scuba dive if you can't swim
- **Secondary:** swimming requirement for scuba diving, scuba diving for non-swimmers
- **Intent:** informational, high-value objection-handling (this stops a lot of people from ever enquiring)
- **Title tag:** `Can You Scuba Dive If You Can't Swim Well? | Deep Dive Trips`
- **Meta description:** `You don't need to be a strong swimmer to try scuba diving. Here's the real swim requirement for beginner dives and certification courses.`
- **Outline:** what certifying bodies (PADI/SSI) actually require for Open Water (a basic swim test + float/tread, not competitive swimming ability), how a Discover Scuba Dive differs (often no swim test at all, fully guided), practical tips for nervous swimmers (mask/fin practice, buoyancy control makes swimming ability less relevant underwater than people assume)
- **Word count:** 700-900
- **Internal links:** → certification course page

### 7. Scuba diving and your ears: how equalizing actually works (and why it matters)
- **Target keyword:** how to equalize ears scuba diving
- **Secondary:** ear pain scuba diving, scuba diving ear problems beginners
- **Intent:** informational — this is consistently one of the top anxiety points for first-time divers and a common reason people bail mid-course
- **Title tag:** `How to Equalize Your Ears While Scuba Diving | Deep Dive Trips`

- **Word count:** 600-800
- **Internal links:** → "what to expect" post, → certification course page
- **CTA:** `[Talk to an instructor before you book →]` (`click_whatsapp`)
