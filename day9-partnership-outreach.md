Goal: build a pipeline of local referral partners who already have a captive audience of the
right traveler (people already booked into a beach town / already paying for adventure travel /
already trusting a hotel concierge's recommendation) — this is consistently cheaper per booking
than paid ads because the referring partner has done the trust-building for you, and it compounds

## Partner category framework

Ranked by expected lead quality × ease of getting a yes. "How to find real prospects" below uses
searches you (or I, once the location is known) can actually run today — not placeholders.

| # | Category | Why it converts | How to find real prospects | Suggested referral structure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hotels, resorts & guesthouses within ~15-20 min of the dive site/dock | Front desk and concierge get asked "what is there to do here" constantly; a diver who's already paid for a room nearby is the single highest-intent lead category that exists. | Google Maps search `hotels near [dive site/dock name]` and `resorts [town name]`, sorted by review count; cross-check against Booking.com/Agoda "things to do" tabs for the same town, which lists which activities they already promote. | 10-15% commission per booking referred, paid within 7 days, OR a simple "we recommend you, you recommend us" reciprocal listing (no cash) for smaller guesthouses — offer both, let them pick. |
| 2 | Independent dive shops / dive centers in *non-competing* locations (different coast, different island, different country even) | Divers travel between dive destinations; a shop in [Location A] gets asked "know anywhere good near [Location B]?" regularly. Zero competitive conflict, purely additive. | PADI Dive Shop Locator (padi.com) and SSI Dive Center Locator (divessi.com) filtered to India + nearby dive geographies (Sri Lanka, Maldives, Thailand for the inbound-foreign-tourist angle); also sccheck Thrillophilia/TripAdvisor "diving" category by other Indian coastal towns (Goa, Lakshadweep, Andaman, Pondicherry, Netrani — pick the ones that aren't Deep Dive Trips' own base). | No commission, informal cross-referral agreement ("we send you divers heading your way, you send us divers heading ours") — lowest friction, easiest yes, worth doing first to build a track record before pitching the harder categories. |
| 3 | Local tour operators / travel agents doing multi-day itineraries that pass through the area | They already sell packages and are actively looking for activity add-ons that make their itinerary look richer without them having to operate it. | JustDial and IndiaMART search `tour operator [city/region]`; also look at who's running "[Region] holiday package" ads/listings on Thrillophilia and Yatra — those are itinerary sellers, not direct competitors. | 10-15% commission, or a fixed per-head add-on rate they can mark up in their package pricing — offer a simple one-page rate card so they can quote instantly without calling every time. |
| 4 | Water-sports operators offering non-diving activities (jet ski, parasailing, snorkeling-only, banana boat) at the same beach/dock | Their customers are already adventure-curious and physically on-site; a snorkeler is one conversation away from a trial dive upsell. | Walk the same beach/dock and ask directly (this category is found in person, not online — most don't have a real web presence); alternatively Google Maps `water sports [beach name]`. | Simple flat referral fee per walk-in they send over (e.g. a fixed amount per confirmed trial dive) — keep it dead simple since these are often small, informal operators. |
| 5 | Wedding/pre-wedding and underwater photographers | Underwater pre-wedding shoots are a real and growing niche in Indian coastal wedding markets; the photographer needs a dive operator for safety/logistics, and couples doing this are high-value, high-referral-potential customers (they show the photos everywhere). | Instagram search `#underwaterweddingindia`, `#prewedding[cityname]`; also ask any wedding photographers already found via Day 6's Instagram outreach work if that overlaps. | Package deal: photographer refers the couple, Deep Dive Trips handles the dive logistics + safety diver, split explicitly agreed per booking rather than a %. |
| 6 | Corporate / MICE (meetings, incentives, conferences, events) planners and team-outing companies | Corporate groups book in bulk, book off-peak (filling weekday capacity), and rebook annually if the first outing goes well. | This category gets its own full outreach list on **Day 14** (Corporate/group booking outreach) — don't duplicate effort here; Day 9 only needs to flag it in the tracking table below so Day 14 has a head start on the same partner-sourcing method. | Deferred to Day 14. |
| 7 | Homestays / hostel chains popular with backpackers (Zostel, Backpacker Panda, The Hosteller, goSTOPS, if any operate in the relevant coastal town) | Backpacker hostels actively curate an "activities board" for guests and often have an existing tie-up model already — easiest single point of contact to get in front of many independent travelers at once. | Check each chain's website for a location in the relevant town; hostels list a manager/activities-coordinator contact on their own site or Instagram. | These chains often want a *listed* partner rate card (fixed discount %) rather than a referral commission — check their standard partner-onboarding process per chain rather than assuming. |

## Outreach sequence (do in this order once real details exist)

1. **Dive shops in non-competing locations (category 2)** first — lowest friction, no money
   involved, builds 3-5 warm relationships fast and gives Deep Dive Trips its first "as recommended
   by [other respected dive center]" social proof line to use in later pitches.
2. **3-5 highest-review-count hotels/resorts near the dive site (category 1)** — the highest-value
   category, worth the extra effort of a phone call to the manager rather than a cold email alone.
3. **2-3 water-sports operators at the same beach (category 4)** — in-person, same trip as an
   on-site visit, cheap to do alongside category 2/3 outreach.
4. **Backpacker hostel chains (category 7)** and **tour operators (category 3)** in parallel once
   the first partnerships are live and there's a real reference to point to.
5. Wedding photographers (category 5) — lower volume but worth one outreach batch once the core
   list above is running, since it needs its own pitch angle.

Target: 8-10 partner conversations in the first outreach round, aiming for 4-5 signed
(even informal) partnerships — outreach conversion for warm, no-cost-to-them offers like category
2 typically runs much higher than cold sales outreach, so this is an achievable first-round target.
